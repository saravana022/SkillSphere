## 📖 About the Project

**SkillSphere** is an innovative platform designed to bridge the gap between **theoretical learning and practical skill development** in colleges.

It transforms traditional clubs into **domain-based learning ecosystems** where students:
- Learn through structured paths  
- Build real-world projects  
- Get mentorship from seniors  
- Track their growth effectively  

📍 **Coimbatore Institute of Engineering and Technology**  
📅 **April 2026**

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

- ❌ No domain-based grouping  
- ❌ Lack of structured learning  
- ❌ No mentorship system  
- ❌ Weak collaboration  
- ❌ Low practical exposure  
- ❌ Poor internship readiness  

📊 Only **43% of students** have mentorship during academics  

---

## 💡 Solution — SkillSphere

✅ Domain-Based Clubs (Web, DevOps, AI/ML)  
✅ Structured Learning Roadmaps  
✅ Mentor-Guided Tasks  
✅ Centralized Progress Tracking  
✅ Real-World Project Building  

---

## ✨ Key Features

🚀 **Task-Based Learning** — Build skills step by step  
🔥 **Streak Tracking** — Stay consistent  
🏆 **Leaderboard System** — Compete & grow  
👥 **Mentor Allocation** — Learn from seniors  
📊 **Progress Tracking** — Measure growth  
🎯 **Domain Grouping** — Focused learning
